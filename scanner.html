What PhishNet AI Evaluates

15 Structural & Lexical Heuristics

Every submitted URL is parsed locally into its constitutional components and evaluated against 15 cybersecurity rules designed to detect social engineering, obfuscation, and brand impersonation tactics:

1. URL Length Anomalies

Detects excessive length used to hide phishing payload destinations.

2. Dots in Hostname

Measures domain segment fragmentation and deep nesting.

3. Hyphens in Hostname

Flags chained security buzzwords (e.g. secure-login-bank-auth).

4. Subdomain Hierarchy

Extracts multi-level subdomains intended to deceive users on mobile viewports.

5. Direct IP Hostnames

Detects IPv4, IPv6, and obfuscated hexadecimal/decimal host targets.

6. Userinfo '@' Spoofing

Identifies embedded credential delimiters exploited in browser routing.

7. Open Redirection Chains

Checks for '//' in paths and secondary URLs injected into query parameters.

8. Punycode & IDN Homographs

Detects 'xn--' prefixes replacing Latin glyphs with lookalike scripts.

9. Obfuscated URL Encoding

Flags hex percent-encoding in hostnames or double-encoding (%25).

10. Inflated Query Strings

Analyzes long serialized parameters carrying credential interception tokens.

11. Non-Standard Ports

Identifies high-numbered ports bypassing standard perimeter firewalls.

12. HTTP Protocol Absence

Warns of unencrypted plaintext channels without assuming HTTPS is safe.

13. Sensitive Keyword Traps

Scans for login, verify, invoice, banking, and security keywords in domains.

14. Deceptive Brand Misalignment

Flags target brands in subdomains when registered domain is unaffiliated.

15. High-Abuse TLDs

Flags domain registries with statistically high malicious exploitation rates.
